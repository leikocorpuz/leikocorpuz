ABOUT ME

Hello! My name is Krysta and I am a 20 year old sophomore in the University of the Philippines Open University taking Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Studies. I'm not only a student, but also a freelancer doing art commissions on VGen. I've always been fascinated by anything digital — programming, animating, drawing, designing, posting, you name it.

During my free time, I enjoy playing games, writing stories, drawing, and — most of all — sleeping. I also very much enjoy cosplaying and collecting merch (that is, if I have spare money).
